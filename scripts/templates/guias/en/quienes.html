About us

tutti.frutti is a free online Stop game (also known as Categories or Tutti Frutti) with no sign-up, made for playing with friends, family or a random opponent from any phone or computer.

How tutti.frutti started

Stop is a game almost all of us learned with pen and paper, at the dinner table or on a long road trip. We wanted it to be just as easy to play from far away, without downloading anything and without endless arguments about whether a word counts.

What matters to us

  • Free and simple. You play from the browser, with no account or password. We only ask for a name for the game.
  • Fair games. Letters are drawn without repeats, the round ends for everyone at the same time and, when there's doubt, an AI judge decides using the same criteria for everyone.
  • Respect for your privacy. We don't sell data, and we explain what information we use in our privacy policy.
